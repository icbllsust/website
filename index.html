    SECTION · 10

    Visa & Travel Support

    Foreign delegates are advised to apply for an appropriate Bangladesh visa at least 8 weeks before the conference. The organising committee will issue an official invitation letter on request.

    Most foreign nationals require a visa to enter Bangladesh. The Government of Bangladesh operates e-Visa, Visa-on-Arrival (for selected nationalities), and embassy-stamped visa regimes. Delegates are responsible for verifying their specific category with the nearest Bangladesh diplomatic mission.

    1. Register on the ICBLLC 2027 portal and obtain your registration confirmation email (required for invitation letter).
    2. Email the secretariat (visa@icbllc2027.sust.edu) with subject 'Invitation Letter — [Full Name] — [Passport No.]' to request the formal invitation letter.
    3. Apply for the appropriate visa category (academic / conference) at the nearest Bangladesh Embassy or via the official e-Visa portal (visa.gov.bd).
    4. Carry the printed invitation letter, registration receipt, and abstract acceptance email to the immigration counter on arrival.

    For invitation letters or visa clarification, write to visa@icbllc2027.sust.edu with a minimum lead time of 6 weeks. The secretariat does not intervene in individual embassy decisions.
